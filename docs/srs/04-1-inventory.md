### 4.1 Inventory Management

**Description:** The Inventory component (`scm_sim.inventory`, class `Inventory`) is the single source of truth for stock. It records the quantity of every SKU held at every warehouse, enforces each registered warehouse's total capacity, stores a stock policy (reorder point, reorder quantity, max level) per (warehouse, SKU) item, and tells the rest of the system which items need replenishment or hold excess stock. The Order Manager and the Shipment Manager change stock only through this component, and the Analytics component reads it only through an independent snapshot.

**Priority:** High. Every other feature depends on correct stock levels.

**Stimulus / response sequences**

| Stimulus | Response |
|---|---|
| A shipment arrives at `WH-NORTH` with 150 `WIDGET` | Stock of (`WH-NORTH`, `WIDGET`) increases by 150, unless that would exceed the warehouse capacity |
| A customer order for 30 `WIDGET` is fulfilled from `WH-NORTH` | Stock of (`WH-NORTH`, `WIDGET`) decreases by 30 |
| The daily replenishment check runs | Inventory returns every tracked item at or below its reorder point together with its reorder quantity |
| The daily report is built | Inventory returns a copy of all stock levels |
