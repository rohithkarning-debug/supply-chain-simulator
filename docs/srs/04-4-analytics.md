### 4.4 Analytics & Reporting

Description: The Analytics and Reporting subsystem derives operational insight from the simulator's plain state snapshots without mutating them. It detects late inbound shipments by comparing expected arrival days against the current or actual delivery day, classifies inventory health into stockouts, low stock and excess inventory against configurable thresholds, aggregates customer order counts and a fill rate, and composes these findings into a single daily report that is rendered as human-readable text for the Supply Chain Analyst. All operations are implemented as pure functions in `scm_sim.analytics` that hold no internal state, depend on no other component, and are invoked once per simulated day by the Simulation Engine as the final step of the daily time-step.
