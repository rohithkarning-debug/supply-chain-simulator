### 4.2 Order Management

**Description:** The Order Management subsystem (`scm_sim.orders`) is responsible for managing customer demand and tracking the end-to-end lifecycle of customer orders within the supply chain simulator. It encapsulates order creation, validation of demand quantities, deterministic sequential identification (`ORD-0001`, `ORD-0002`, ...), status progression across `PENDING`, `FULFILLED`, `BACKORDERED`, and `CANCELLED`, atomic full-order fulfillment against warehouse inventory without partial shipments, prioritized batch retry of open orders on a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) basis, order retrieval by identifier, and state-guarded order cancellation.
